== Installing a printer

Before you can install a printer, you need to know the `+root+` password and have your printer information ready. Depending on how you connect the printer, you might also need the printer URI, TCP/IP address or host, and the driver for the printer. Common printer drivers ship with {productname}. If you cannot find a driver for the printer, check the printer manufacturer's Web site.

. Make sure the CUPS printing service is available and automatically started at boot time:
+
----
> sudo systemctl enable cups.service
> sudo systemctl start cups.service
----
. Start the printer configuration by right-clicking the desktop and choosing *Settings* and then *Printers*.
. Click *Unlock* in the upper right corner and enter the `+root+` password.
. Click the *Add* button.
. If there are too many printers in the list, filter them by entering an IP address or a keyword into the search field in the lower part of the dialog.
. Select a printer from the list of available printers and click *Add*.

The installed printer appears in the Printers panel. You can now print to the printer from any application.

=== Installing a local printer

Connect the printer cable to your computer and connect the printer's power supply. Your system can recognize many types of printers once they are connected.

If your printer was not recognized automatically, proceed as follows:

. Open the *Activities* overview and start typing `+Printers+`.
+
Click *Printers*. You may have to press *Unlock* in the top-right corner and type in your `+root+` password.
. Press the *Add* button.
. In the pop-up window, select your printer and press *Add*.

The installed printer appears in the Printers dialog. You can now print to the printer from any application.

== Modifying printer settings

. Open the *Activities* overview and start typing `+Printers+`.
. Click *Printers* to open the panel.
. Depending on your system, you may have to press *Unlock* in the top-right corner and type in your `+root+` password.
. Click the *Settings* button next to the printer.
. Select *Printer Details*.
. Modify the printer settings to your needs and close the dialog.

== Canceling print jobs

If you accidentally started a print job, you can cancel the print to save paper and ink.

. Open the *Activities* overview and start typing `+Printers+`.
. Click *Printers* to open the panel.
. Click the *Show Jobs* button on the right hand-side of the *Printers* dialog.
+
If the print job does not appear in the list, it may be printed already.
. Cancel the print job by clicking the *Stop* button.
